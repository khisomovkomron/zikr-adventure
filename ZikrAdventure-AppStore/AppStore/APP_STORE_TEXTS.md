## English

**Subtitle:**
Zikr counter & the Sira path

**Promotional Text:**
Count “Astaghfirullah” and walk the Sira path: 7 levels and 19 chapters about the life of the Prophet ﷺ across maps of 7th-century Mecca and Medina. No ads.

**Keywords:**
zikr,dhikr,astaghfirullah,tasbih,counter,islam,sira,seerah,mecca,medina,makkah,kaaba,muslim,dua

**Description:**

Zikr Adventure is a calm counter for the zikr “Astaghfirullah” (أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ — “I ask Allah for forgiveness”) and a journey through Mecca and Medina in the time of the Prophet ﷺ.

ZIKR
• A large button for every repetition and a clear counter
• Arabic text, transliteration and translation
• Correct a mistaken tap or reset the counter
• Today’s count, daily history and an optional personal goal
• Haptic feedback and a keep-screen-on mode

THE SIRA PATH
• 7 levels from 100 to 100,000,000 repetitions — each ten times longer than the last
• 19 chapters about the Prophet’s ﷺ life in order of events: the valley of Mecca, the first revelation, the Hijra, Medina, the Farewell Pilgrimage
• Isometric maps of 7th-century Mecca and Medina: the Kaaba, Maqam Ibrahim, Zamzam, Safa and Marwa, the caves of Hira and Thawr, the Prophet’s ﷺ Mosque, Quba, al-Qiblatayn, al-Baqi, Uhud and more
• For every place: its significance, its appearance in the 7th century and its link to the Sira
• Along the path the city around the sacred places comes alive: caravans, houses, lamps, palm groves, the route of the Hijra

WITH RESPECT
• Sacred places are always visible and open — they are never prizes
• The count is not a measure of spiritual worth
• People and the Prophet ﷺ are not depicted

LANGUAGES
English, Русский, O‘zbekcha, Тоҷикӣ, Қазақша, Кыргызча.

PRIVACY

## Notes for App Review (заметки для проверяющего, на английском)

```
Zikr Adventure is a fully offline app. No account or login is required.

How to test:
1. On the first launch choose a language and tap "Begin" (or "Skip").
2. Tap the large round button to count the zikr "Astaghfirullah".
   "Correct" removes one tap, "Reset" sets the counter to zero (with a confirmation).
3. Open the "Map" tab → choose Mecca or Medina → "Open city map".
   The isometric map can be moved with one finger and zoomed with two fingers.
   Tap any "i" marker to read about a historical place.
4. The "Sira path" tab shows 7 levels and 19 chapters. Chapters open at repetition
   milestones (the first one at 10 taps). Information about every place is available from the start.
5. Settings: language (6 languages), vibration, sound, animations, keep screen on,
   optional daily goal, reset of all progress.

Native features: haptic feedback (UIFeedbackGenerator), keep-screen-awake during zikr
(isIdleTimerDisabled), on-device storage of progress. All content is bundled in the app;
the app makes no network requests and collects no data.
```
