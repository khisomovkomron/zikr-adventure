Zikr Adventure 1.2.0 — Native SwiftUI Preview

The app has been rebuilt with native screens while keeping the existing experience and local progress.

Please install this beta over your current App Store installation without deleting the app. Before updating, note your total count, daily history, language/settings and unlocked chapters. Verify that they remain after updating and after restarting the app.

Please test:
- Counting, undo, both reset options, daily goal, history and a change of day.
- First-launch onboarding, Skip, language selection and completed-onboarding persistence.
- All tabs, chapter unlocking/reading, viewed markers, map navigation, saved camera and place cards.
- Google and Apple sign-in, cancellation, session restoration, sign-out and account deletion with reauthentication. Check Apple sign-in revocation after deletion.
- All six languages, light/dark mode, larger text, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion.

Signing in does not provide cloud progress synchronization or restoration on another device. Account sign-out/deletion should preserve local progress.

Report the build number, iPhone model, iOS version, language, steps, expected/actual results and screenshots. Do not include passwords, tokens or private account details.
